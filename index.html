Important · please read before you play

This is a demonstration, not an assessment

Learning Rate Lab is a scientifically grounded demonstration of how people learn from experience. It's built to help you understand your learning mind, never to judge it.

What this game is not

  • ✕Not a test of intelligence, ability, or personality.
  • ✕Not a diagnosis. It says nothing about your mental health and isn't a substitute for a professional.
  • ✕Not a fixed label. Results from one short game naturally vary from day to day.
No right or wrong answers Play the way that feels natural. There's no correct style to aim for.
Every mind is unique Steady, balanced, and quick learners each have real strengths. Your result describes how you played today, not who you are.
Grounded in research The task and the learning model come from decades of research on how people update their beliefs.
